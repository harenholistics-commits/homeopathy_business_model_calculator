Pick your ONE stream & plan the season

Which stream takes the least of ME per dollar? Put in the streams you starred, rate how much of you each one takes, add what you'd charge for one & how many you'd do a month & it ranks them & plots them on a grid. Top-right corner is the one to build first. Then pick your ONE & write your brief game plan for the season. Hit the button & you get a one-page PDF plus a ready-to-paste post for the community space.

1. Your starred streams

Add the two to five streams you starred in activity two. "How much of ME" is the honest one: 1 = barely costs me anything, 5 = drains me. Then two easy numbers. Price of one sale: what one customer pays you one time for this stream, so one custom blend, one kit, one seat in a class, one month of a membership, one wholesale order. Sales a month: how many of those you'd realistically do in a month. The dollars a month works itself out from those two. Hours a month is your rough guess of the time it takes. Same math as Module 2: real time, real costs.

2. Which takes the least of ME per dollar?

This updates as you type. Dollars a month divided by how much of ME it takes is the ranking. The grid puts "least of me" on the right & "most dollars" at the top, so top-right is the one to build first. Then choose your ONE below. It doesn't have to be the top of the list, it has to be the one you'll actually do.

3. My brief game plan for the season

The workbook checklist asks for this & there's no page for it, so here it is. Keep it short. One season is about 90 days. Live in it, notice & adjust. You are allowed to change!

Nothing here is saved anywhere but your PDF, so download it. Bring the Hub post to the community space if you'd like input.
