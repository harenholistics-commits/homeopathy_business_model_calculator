8. Before you offer this

  • Cell salts & Bach essences are sold over the counter with the manufacturer's label. You are recommending a labeled product, not making a remedy. Use the label wording when you say what it's for.
  • If you stock & sell the bottles yourself, your state will likely want a sales tax or reseller permit, & wholesalers like Faire will ask for it. That's state by state, so check your own state & your Module 1 notes. Fullscript orders are Fullscript's sale, not yours.
  • If you sell any product you are dispensing. Check your product liability coverage from Lesson 1.4.
  • Keep it to short-term situations. Anything chronic, anything that worries you, or anyone in crisis gets a full consult or a referral.
  • No claims to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent on your form, your site or your reply. Say what the product is labeled for.
