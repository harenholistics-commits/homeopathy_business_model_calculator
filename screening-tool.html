Build your own screening tool

This is my exact screening form, the one you watched me walk through in the Loom, laid out so you can make it yours. Keep the questions you want, reword any of them, switch off the ones that don't fit how you practice & add your own. Hit the button at the bottom & you'll get a copy-&-paste block that Typeform turns into a form for you, plus the steps to do it.

This is my screening tool. This is to make sure that all the clients that apply to work with me are really in alignment with who I want to work with, that we're going to be a good fit for each other. I used to offer full-blown discovery calls. Here's the thing, people would not show up to these discovery calls. So I just had a lot of wasted time when I was doing discovery calls & that's why I don't do that anymore. So I put this screening tool in here in place of it.

1. The basics

Used for the form title & the booking screen at the end. Both optional.

2. What they read before the questions

Mine, with my numbers in [brackets] for you to swap. Say it your way. Delete anything you don't offer.

3. My screening questions

Tick the ones you want. Reword anything. "Not a fit if" marks the answer that should end the form before they can book (you add that one logic jump inside Typeform after the import, the steps are on your results page).

4. Your own screening questions

What do you need to know before someone sits in front of you? Pets only? Kids? A certain condition you focus on? Budget? Timezone? Add as many as you like. Nobody but you sees this page.

5. What they see at the end

Two endings: one for the people who pass & one for the people who don't.

Typeform's "Import questions" box takes up to 6,000 characters. The counter above tells you where you're at. A free Typeform account allows 10 questions per form & 10 responses a month, so if you keep every question & add your own, you may need a paid plan (or trim).
