Build your own screening tool

This is Haren Holistics' exact screening tool, the one Lexie walks through in the Loom, laid out so you can make it yours. Keep the questions you want, reword any of them, switch off the ones that don't fit how you practice & add your own. Hit the button at the bottom & you'll get your questions as one copy-&-paste block, plus the steps to turn it into a form in Typeform, Google Forms or whatever builder you use.

1. The basics

Name & booking link are optional. The form builder decides which steps you get on your results page.

2. What they read before the questions

Haren Holistics' exact wording, with Lexie's numbers in [brackets] for you to swap. Say it your way. Delete anything you don't offer.

3. Haren Holistics' screening questions

Tick the ones you want. Reword anything. "Not a fit if" marks the answer that should end the form before they can book (you add that one logic jump inside Typeform after the import, the steps are on your results page).

4. Your own screening questions

What do you need to know before someone sits in front of you? Pets only? Kids? A certain condition you focus on? Budget? Timezone? Add as many as you like. Nobody but you sees this page.

5. What they see at the end

Two endings: one for the people who pass & one for the people who don't.
